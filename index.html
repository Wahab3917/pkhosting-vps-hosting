Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are charged for ten months instead of twelve, so two months are free. The full year is invoiced once, up front, and the per-month figure shown on annual cards is that yearly total divided by twelve.

Which currency will I actually be charged in?

Every invoice is issued in Pakistani rupees. US dollar and pound sterling figures are shown for reference at a fixed internal rate and are not a live market quote, so what you see here is what appears on the invoice.

Can I move between plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with the remaining balance credited against the new plan. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period so you keep the resources you have already paid for.

Do I get full root access?

All four plans include full root access over SSH, your own kernel, and a console you can reach even if the network configuration is broken. A control panel is available if you would rather not work at the command line.

Where are the servers located?

The VPS fleet is hosted in Karachi with an onward peering route through Dubai. For visitors inside Pakistan this keeps round-trip latency well below what an equivalent server in Europe or North America can offer.

What happens if I cancel mid-term?

Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next renewal date with no fee. Annual plans are refundable on a pro-rata basis for the unused whole months, less the two months that were given free at the start of the term.
